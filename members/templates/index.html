Why Donate Blood?

One donation can save up to three lives. Blood cannot be manufactured — it can only come from generous donors like you. Your contribution directly helps patients in your community.

  • Takes only 45–60 minutes of your time
  • Safe, supervised process — your health is our priority
  • Donors receive a free health screening & blood test report
  • You can donate again every 56 days (whole blood)
